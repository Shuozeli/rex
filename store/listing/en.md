## Description

rex turns the page you are reading into one structured JSON record, with one click, inside your browser.

Click the rex icon on an article, a list, a product, a video or a stock quote. rex reads the open page with a recipe for that site, or with the schema.org data the page publishes, and shows the record: an article's title, authors, dates and text; the items of a list; a product's price; a video's channel and length; a quote's price and figures.

• Reader and Table views, with the exact JSON at the bottom.
• Copy JSON or download a .json file: the full result with where each value came from, or the record alone.
• More than 1,000 recipes built in: about 40 popular sites (Wikipedia, Hacker News, GitHub, Stack Overflow, Reddit, YouTube, Amazon, Yahoo Finance and others) and hundreds of news sites. Pages that publish schema.org data, such as articles, recipes, products, events, jobs, books and apps, work through generic recipes.
• Works offline. The recipes are inside the extension. rex never connects to the internet, sends nothing anywhere, and asks for no access to websites: it reads a tab only when you click its icon.
• English and Chinese interface.
• For recipe authors, developer mode shows how each field was found, runs a recipe you paste in, and saves a copy of the page with its text replaced, for tests.
